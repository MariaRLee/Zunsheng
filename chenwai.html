The concluding lines of Gao Lian’s opening remarks frame the lives of reclusive figures through freedom from striving and worldly disturbance. Readers may reflect on values and the meaning of a good life without assuming withdrawal from society is the only right choice.

白話一句話In Plain Language

不被無止境的追逐與外在紛擾牽引，內心才較能安然自在。Freedom from constant striving and intrusion is associated with inner ease in the passage.

核心主旨Core Message

《塵外遐舉箋》透過高隱人物與逸志思想，探討人在不同處境中如何保有志節與自主。The volume situates ideals of freedom and integrity within historical accounts of reclusive figures.

三個閱讀重點Three Key Insights

  1. 隱逸是古代特定歷史文化中的人生選擇。Reclusion reflects a particular historical context.
  2. 追求自我價值不必完全依靠他人肯定。External approval is not the sole measure of value.
  3. 不宜將隱居視為每個人都應採取的生活方式。Withdrawal is not a universal prescription.

經典與文化脈絡Classical & Cultural Context

網站說明 · 計畫宗旨About this project

《遵生八箋：古典智慧與美好永續生活》以明代高濂著作為基礎，希望讓讀者從古籍中學習如何過更美好的人生。網站兼顧學術可溯源與一般大眾的閱讀體驗。Zunsheng Bajian brings Gao Lian’s Ming-dynasty text to modern readers, encouraging thoughtful reflection on better living through accessible, traceable classical sources.

全站依原著結構呈現六箋、十五卷，暫不納入《延年卻病箋》與《靈秘丹藥箋》。已校對的選段提供古籍原文、完整的選段白話翻譯、英文精選導讀、文化背景與生活反思；未完成的卷次明確標示。The site covers six collections and fifteen original volumes, excluding the two medicine and alchemy collections for now. Edited excerpts include classical Chinese, full modern-Chinese translation of those excerpts, English highlights, historical notes and modern reflections. Unfinished material is clearly marked.

They are modern editorial interpretations, not Gao Lian’s original categories or modern medical evidence.
