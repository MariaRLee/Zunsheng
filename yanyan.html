古籍原文（節錄）Classical Chinese · Excerpt

若以無所有者，則手執一枝，或採滿把，即插之水鉢壁縫。

繁體白話翻譯（上述選段）Modern Chinese · Translation of Excerpt

如果一個人沒有名貴的花器，也可以手持一枝花、採集一把花枝，甚至插在水鉢或牆縫裡欣賞。

English Highlights · 英文精選導讀Selected English Guide

In the discussion of vase flowers, Gao Lian also recognizes the pleasure of arranging flowers without expensive vessels. This provides a fruitful point of departure for reflecting on accessible beauty and the continuing practice of traditional arts.

古今對話 · 生活反思Wisdom for Today · Reflection

反思問題Reflection Question

如何欣賞日常事物的美，並理解傳統藝術背後的文化精神？Can simple objects be beautiful without being expensive?

參考思考方向Suggested Interpretation

這段文字讓我們看見，賞花並不必以昂貴器物為前提。理解花枝、容器與空間的關係，可以更細緻地體會傳統文人美學；這是根據原文提出的現代詮釋。Consider form, context and care rather than price; traditional flower art offers one historical example.

觀察窗邊一株植物或一件生活器物，想想它的形態、材質與位置如何帶來美感。請勿為練習而採摘受保護或不適合採集的植物。Suggested modern practice (see Chinese text for details).

網站說明 · 計畫宗旨About this project

《遵生八箋：古典智慧與美好永續生活》以明代高濂著作為基礎，希望讓讀者從古籍中學習如何過更美好的人生。網站兼顧學術可溯源與一般大眾的閱讀體驗。Zunsheng Bajian brings Gao Lian’s Ming-dynasty text to modern readers, encouraging thoughtful reflection on better living through accessible, traceable classical sources.

全站依原著結構呈現六箋、十五卷，暫不納入《延年卻病箋》與《靈秘丹藥箋》。已校對的選段提供古籍原文、完整的選段白話翻譯、英文精選導讀、文化背景與生活反思；未完成的卷次明確標示。The site covers six collections and fifteen original volumes, excluding the two medicine and alchemy collections for now. Edited excerpts include classical Chinese, full modern-Chinese translation of those excerpts, English highlights, historical notes and modern reflections. Unfinished material is clearly marked.

五大生活視角：身心幸福（包含 SEL）、生活美學、自然與生活永續、人生智慧、文化永續。這些是現代的詮釋工具，不是高濂原有的章節分類，也不代表古代養生說法獲得現代醫學證實。The five optional reading lenses are Well-being & SEL, Art & Aesthetics, Sustainable Living, Life Wisdom, and Cultural Sustainability. They are modern editorial interpretations, not Gao Lian’s original categories or modern medical evidence.
